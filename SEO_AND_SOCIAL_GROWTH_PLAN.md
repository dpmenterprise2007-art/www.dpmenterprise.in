# DPM Enterprise — Free SEO & Social Growth Implementation Plan

Audit date: 2026-11-19
Canonical public site reviewed: https://www.dpmenterprise.in/
Business: DPM ENTERPRISE PRIVATE LIMITED
Primary market: Virar, Vasai, Nalasopara, Mumbai, Maharashtra; qualified pan-India corporate, institutional and residential enquiries.

## Verified public-page observations

The homepage currently communicates interior design, turnkey contracting, corporate/residential/defence-government work, modular kitchens, furniture manufacturing and Virar/Mumbai contact details.

The following visible claims require owner-side evidence before they are used in SEO landing pages or social posts:
- "Free site visit" / "free consultation" and "48 hour" site-visit promises.
- "100% on-time delivery", "10-year warranty", "GeM Star Seller", "Defence Empanelled Vendor", award claims and client/review/testimonial attributions.
- Counts such as 500+ projects, 13,000+ enquiries, 876+ projects and 4.9 / 500+ reviews. Ensure definitions, dates and evidence match.
- The live page currently has conflicting project totals (500+ and 876+) and review totals (4.9 / 500+ and 4.8 / 10).

## Mandatory commercial correction

Do not promise a free site visit. The approved commercial rule is a site-visit fee of INR 5,000 + 18% GST (INR 5,900 total). Explain scope and payment terms clearly before booking. Never imply that an AI assistant can waive this fee.

## Free technical SEO worklist

1. Confirm one canonical HTTPS hostname (www or non-www) and 301-redirect the alternate host.
2. Publish and validate /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml; include only canonical, indexable URLs and accurate last-modified dates.
3. Add unique title and meta description to each service/location page; avoid duplicating the homepage across cities.
4. Add one descriptive H1 per page, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, descriptive internal links and meaningful image alt text.
5. Add Organization/LocalBusiness, Service, BreadcrumbList and FAQ structured data only where the visible page supports the facts. Validate JSON-LD and avoid fabricated ratings/reviews.
6. Set Open Graph and social preview metadata; add canonical URLs, language, viewport and favicon.
7. Compress and resize images, prefer modern WebP/AVIF where supported, lazy-load below-the-fold media, specify image dimensions and reduce unused JavaScript/CSS.
8. Check mobile usability, HTTPS, broken links, 404/redirect chains, Core Web Vitals and accessibility.
9. Create distinct, useful service pages: Corporate Office Interiors, Residential Interiors, Modular Kitchens, Custom Furniture Manufacturing, Turnkey Interiors, Government/Institutional Interiors.
10. Create location pages only where DPM has genuine service coverage and useful unique information. Do not mass-generate thin city doorway pages.

## Free measurement and indexing

- Google Search Console: verify domain ownership, submit sitemap, inspect canonical/indexing and monitor queries.
- Google Business Profile: keep name, address, phone, categories, hours, services and photos consistent; request genuine reviews without incentives.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: verify the site and submit sitemap.
- Use Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights and Search Console reports for diagnostics. These tools do not guarantee rankings.

## Organic social publishing plan

Suggested cadence (IST): 09:00, 13:00, 18:00, subject to content readiness and audience response.
- 09:00: educational design tip, material/finish explainer, checklist or before/after with client permission.
- 13:00: project process, BOQ/costing education, manufacturing detail, site execution or team craft.
- 18:00: completed project story, service explainer, FAQ, enquiry CTA or testimonial with documented consent.

Repurpose each original post for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile with platform-appropriate copy. Use original DPM-approved logo asset unchanged; no AI recreation, recolouring or redesign. Keep the supplied logo small (maximum 5% of image area).

## Editorial and compliance safeguards

- No fake projects, customer names, reviews, certifications, defence/government endorsements, rankings or performance guarantees.
- Get written permission before posting client/site images, logos, faces, testimonials or sensitive project details.
- No unsolicited bulk messaging, scraped private contact data or automated engagement spam.
- Keep an approval queue. Auto-publishing requires authorized platform account connection, valid platform permissions and tokens; never store secrets in source control.
- Organic reach, indexing and rankings cannot be guaranteed. No paid promotion is required for the worklist above.

## Automation architecture

Recommended workflow: draft -> factual/brand checks -> human approval -> scheduled publish -> platform result capture -> UTM-tagged website visits -> CRM lead attribution -> monthly performance review.

A social scheduler or posting API must not be described as connected until platform authorization succeeds and a real test post is confirmed. Instagram/Facebook publishing permissions and LinkedIn organization permissions depend on account type and platform review/policy.

## Release gates before calling this complete

- Website source repository and production hosting/deployment target identified.
- Technical checks pass on the live canonical host (robots, sitemap, canonical, metadata, structured data, redirects, mobile performance).
- Search Console/Bing verification and sitemap submission confirmed.
- Every public factual claim checked against company-approved evidence.
- Social platform connections authorized; one test post per platform confirmed before enabling schedules.
- Analytics and CRM attribution tested end to end.
